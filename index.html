How to play 🎮

Wally has six laboratory stations. Every station is a real science mystery you solve by changing something and watching the result.

01Choose an object

Select a metal or material from the tray. Notice its appearance and properties.

02Make your prediction

Before testing, tell Wally what you think will happen. Guesses are welcome!

03Run the experiment

Weigh, drop, magnetise, heat, connect or shape. See the object actually change.

04Use what you discovered

Test another material, then solve a real-life problem to unlock the next station.

💡 Helpful science tip: A prediction can be wrong. That is how scientists learn! Wally will explain what happened, and you can test again. Heat and electrical experiments here are virtual—do not copy them with household electricity or hot appliances.
